HOST A GAME

Hosting from a phone works too: the match runs right here with touch controls on top. Keep the screen on and this tab open while you play.

This computer runs the match and gets a 4-letter room code. Friends join from their own computers with the code. People in the room with you can also play here on the keyboard, a game controller or a phone.

JOIN A GAME

Type the room code from the host’s screen. You play with your keyboard, or link your phone as the controller once you’re in.

On a phone with no computer? You can play right here: the game fills the screen and the touch controls sit on top. Turn the phone sideways. It’s cramped, so a computer or a phone controller is better when you have one.

Everyone in one room with one big screen? The local version works best for that. This online version connects computers directly, so it needs no game server; a few strict school or work networks block that kind of link.
